I believe the college period is a significant phase in one's life, not only because we secure employment after graduation but, more importantly, because we acquire essential life skills. My primary goal is therefore to help my students make the most of this time.

My teaching method is characterized by building student confidence through the material, progressively challenging them with new concepts, and confronting them with appropriately difficult problems toward the end of the course. My primary function in this process is to initiate students into knowledge building and guide their exploration of new ideas, expanding their existing capacity to learn. Ultimately, I aim to create an engaging and active learning environment in which they develop mental toughness, critical thinking, and disciplined perspectives on real life.

## Evaluation

The degree of difficulty in my students' evaluations is inversely proportional to the rigour of their learning process. This is not because my exams are easier, but partly because of their deeper learning and partly because of my liberal approach to marking. I believe grading is not an exact science, and a teacher cannot consistently and fairly evaluate student performance. To tackle this challenge, I use grading matrices that communicate performance indicators and their corresponding numerical values to students clearly. A substantial portion of the total mark is assigned to content, thereby encouraging students to achieve the primary learning outcomes.

## Continuum  

I consider mastering a profession a continuum, not a series of schooling experiences that end with a vocational or college program. I therefore promote lifelong learning by helping my students transition from their time with me to their next endeavour. Ingrained in my courses are elements of self-directed and web-based learning, both of which I hope will help my students construct their own learning paths.

## Technology Integration

Technology is a key element of my teaching approach, not as a replacement for sound pedagogy but as a tool to enhance it. I use learning management systems, simulations, and engineering software to support diverse learning needs and increase engagement. My experience with online and hybrid delivery has underscored the importance of clarity and structure. I also emphasise digital literacy to prepare students for the thoughtful and effective use of technology in their professional lives.
